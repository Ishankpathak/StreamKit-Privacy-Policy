StreamKit Privacy Policy

1. Data stored on your device

The app stores your browsing history, bookmarks, open tabs, home shortcuts, settings and a blocked-items counter locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. Incognito tabs do not save history or tab thumbnails. You can clear history and bookmarks in Settings, or remove all app data by uninstalling the app.

2. Websites you visit

The app loads websites you choose using the Android WebView. Those websites, and any third parties they load, may collect data under their own privacy policies (for example your IP address, cookies and device information). We do not control or receive this data.

3. Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and approximate location to show and measure ads. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app shows a consent form before personalised ads are used. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads). Learn more: Google advertising policy.

4. Premium purchases

Premium purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to verify and manage purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase and subscription information, and device and IP information. We never receive your payment card details. Learn more: RevenueCat privacy policy.

5. Ad-blocking rules

The app may periodically download a list of advertising and tracker domains from a public server. This request exposes your IP address to that server. It contains no other personal information about you.

6. Permissions

  • Internet: to load websites.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to keep audio playing and show a playback notification when the app is in the background.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers described above: Google (AdMob and Google Play) and RevenueCat.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Purchase records are held by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own retention policies. To request deletion of data held by RevenueCat or by us, contact us at the email address above.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Security

Local data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Communication with purchase and advertising services uses encrypted connections.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, email us at pathakishank4@gmail.com.
